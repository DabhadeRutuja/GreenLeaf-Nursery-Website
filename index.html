Plant care made simple

A few easy habits keep your plants thriving all year round.

💧

Watering

  • Check the top 2 cm of soil. Water only when it feels dry.
  • Water deeply until it drains out of the bottom, then empty the saucer.
  • Succulents need far less water than leafy plants.
☀️

Sunlight

  • Bright, indirect light suits most indoor plants.
  • Flowering plants and succulents love several hours of sun.
  • Rotate pots every week so growth stays even.
🌱

Soil

  • Use a light, well-draining potting mix with compost.
  • Add sand or perlite for cacti and succulents.
  • Pick pots with drainage holes to protect the roots.
✂️

Maintenance

  • Trim yellow or dry leaves to encourage fresh growth.
  • Wipe dusty leaves gently with a damp cloth.
  • Repot every 1 to 2 years when roots fill the pot.
🧪

Feeding

  • Feed with diluted organic fertiliser every 3 to 4 weeks in the growing season.
  • Pause feeding in winter when growth slows.
  • Never fertilise a dry or stressed plant.
🐛

Pests and health

  • Look under leaves each week for tiny pests.
  • Wash off pests with a neem-oil spray or mild soapy water.
  • Keep new plants apart for a week before grouping them.
